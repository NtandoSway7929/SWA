Questions, answered

Before you get started.

What does R250–R750 per month include?

The monthly fee depends on the agreed update and support scope. The exact inclusions, limits and response expectations are confirmed in writing before the arrangement starts.

Does maintenance include a redesign?

No. A redesign or substantial new feature is a separate project unless expressly included.

Can you maintain a website built by someone else?

We first need to review the platform, access and technical condition. We will confirm whether the site can be supported before agreeing a fee.

Are hosting and domain renewals included?

Not by default. Hosting, domain renewals, premium plugins and other third-party charges are separate unless the agreement says otherwise.

Can unused monthly updates roll over?

Rollover rules, if any, must be stated in the written maintenance agreement. They are not assumed.

Does maintenance guarantee the website will never go offline?

No. Maintenance can help address agreed issues but cannot guarantee uninterrupted service or prevent every third-party outage or security event.
