Questions, answered

Before you get started.

Is website maintenance compulsory?

No. Maintenance is optional. You can manage your website yourself or select one of the monthly plans.

What counts as a minor update?

Examples include replacing supplied text or images, updating contact details or making a small correction to existing content. New pages, redesigns, new functionality, complex layout changes and large batches of product uploads are not minor updates.

Does the plan include hosting and domain renewal?

No, not by default. Hosting, domain renewals, premium plugins, software licences and third-party subscriptions are separate unless your written agreement expressly includes them.

What if I need more work than my plan includes?

We will explain what falls outside the monthly allowance and provide a separate quote for your approval before carrying out the additional work.

Do unused update minutes carry forward?

No. The monthly time allowance applies to that billing month and does not roll over.

Can you maintain any website?

We first review the platform, hosting arrangement, available access and condition of the website. Some platforms or unsupported configurations may limit what we can safely maintain.
