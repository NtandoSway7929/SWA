Questions, answered

Before you get started.

What does the R500 include?

The R500 service price includes the standard CIPC registration and certificate-related costs covered by this service. Optional or additional third-party services are separate and must be agreed before they are incurred.

Does registration guarantee that CIPC will approve the application?

No. CIPC controls the registration process and outcome. We assist with the administrative steps but cannot guarantee approval or processing time.

What information will I need to provide?

The required information depends on the company and applicable CIPC process. We will provide a checklist for the details needed before submission.

Do you provide legal or tax advice?

No. This is administrative registration assistance, not legal, accounting or tax advice.

Can you help after registration?

We can discuss separate business services where relevant. Ongoing statutory, tax and accounting obligations should be confirmed with the appropriate professional or authority.

How long does registration take?

Processing times are determined by CIPC and can vary. We do not promise a completion date.
