Why booking systems matter
Customers should not have to wait for a message reply just to understand how to book an appointment.

When every appointment starts with a conversation, both sides spend time confirming services, available times and basic details. Requests can become harder to keep organised as messages build up.

A suitable booking flow brings service information, availability and booking instructions into one process. It can reduce repeated admin and gives customers a clearer way to request or schedule a time, depending on the platform configuration.

Questions, answered

What to know before booking setup.

Will customers be able to book online?

The service is intended to configure an online booking journey. The exact customer experience depends on the selected platform and the features included in its plan.

Can the system send confirmations and reminders?

Confirmation and reminder functionality is part of the intended setup where supported by the selected platform. Availability may depend on the provider and subscription plan.

Do I need to pay for a separate booking platform?

Some platforms offer free plans, while others charge subscriptions or transaction fees. Any relevant third-party costs should be confirmed before you commit to a platform.

Can the booking link be added to my website?

We can discuss adding the booking link to your website or other relevant customer touchpoints. Any deeper integration depends on the platform and project scope.

How much does setup cost?

The setup price is R600–R1,200, depending on the requirements and configuration involved.

What do you need from me?

We will ask for your service list, appointment lengths, working hours, availability rules and any booking restrictions. You confirm these details; we configure the agreed flow using the features supported by the selected platform.

What if the platform does not support a feature I need?

We check the platform and plan capabilities before setup begins. We will explain any limitation and agree an alternative or revised scope with you rather than promise a feature the platform cannot provide.
