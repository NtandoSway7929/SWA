Why booking systems matter
Every appointment should not have to begin with a long message thread.

When customers need to ask which services are available, what times are open and how to confirm an appointment, routine messages can take up time on both sides. Details can also become harder to track when requests arrive through several channels.

A suitable online booking flow brings service information, availability and appointment requests into a more organised process. The experience depends on the selected platform and the features included in its plan.

Who it's for

For businesses that run on appointments.

This service is a good fit for salons, barbers, beauty professionals, wellness providers, consultants, tutors, repair services and other appointment-led businesses.

It is especially relevant when appointments are currently arranged through repeated calls or messages, or when the business needs a clearer way to present services and available times. We confirm your scheduling requirements before recommending a platform.

Questions, answered

Before you get started.

Will the system guarantee more bookings or fewer no-shows?

No. The setup gives customers a clearer way to request or schedule appointments. Booking volume depends on demand, your offer and how customers find you. Reminders may support communication where available, but cannot guarantee attendance.

Can customers book online?

The service is intended to configure an online booking journey. The exact experience depends on the selected platform and the features included in its plan.

Can the system send confirmations and reminders?

We can configure available confirmation and reminder functions where the selected platform and subscription plan support them.

Are platform subscriptions or transaction fees included?

No. The R600–R1,200 is for Swayphics setup. Any platform subscription or transaction charges are separate and should be confirmed with the provider before you commit.

Can the booking link be added to my website or Google profile?

We can discuss adding the booking link to your website or other customer touchpoints. Any deeper integration depends on the platform and the agreed project scope.

What do you need before setup?

We will confirm your service list, appointment durations, working hours, availability rules and the information you need from customers. You do not need to choose a platform before the first discussion.
