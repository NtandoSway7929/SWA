Why booking systems matter
Give customers a clear booking route and give yourself fewer details to chase.

A customer should be able to understand what they can book, see the available options and know what happens after they submit a request. When each step depends on a manual reply, the process can become harder to manage as enquiries increase.

We configure the booking flow around your actual service list, schedule and booking rules. The aim is to make the steps clearer for customers and more organised for you, without promising features the selected platform or plan does not support.

Who it's for

For appointment-led businesses ready to simplify scheduling.

This service is designed for appointment-led businesses, particularly salons, nail and beauty professionals, wellness providers, consultants and tutors that offer services customers can schedule in advance.

It is most useful when your services, availability and booking rules can be organised into a repeatable process. We first check how you currently manage appointments and what the selected platform can support.

Questions, answered

What to know before booking setup.

Will customers be able to book online?

The service is intended to configure an online booking journey. The exact customer experience depends on the selected platform and the features included in its plan.

Can the system send confirmations and reminders?

Confirmation and reminder functionality is part of the intended setup where supported by the selected platform. Availability may depend on the provider and subscription plan.

Do I need to pay for a separate booking platform?

Some platforms offer free plans, while others charge subscriptions or transaction fees. Any relevant third-party costs should be confirmed before you commit to a platform.

Can the booking link be added to my website?

We can discuss adding the booking link to your website or other relevant customer touchpoints. Any deeper integration depends on the platform and project scope.

How much does setup cost?

The current Swayphics setup price range is R600–R1,200, depending on the requirements and configuration involved.
