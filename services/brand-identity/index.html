Why identity matters
Give your business one visual language, from its first impression to its everyday documents.

People may meet your business on a social profile, business card, quote, invoice or website. When those materials do not feel related, recognition is harder to build and the business can appear less considered than the work it delivers.

A coherent identity gives you a logo, visual direction and agreed materials that work together. We begin with what your business needs now, not an oversized package of assets you may never use.

Who it's for

For new businesses and growing brands that need consistency.

This service is for new or growing small businesses that need a clear visual foundation, and established businesses whose logo and materials have become inconsistent.

It is especially useful when you are preparing to launch, refreshing an existing identity or need your logo and business materials to feel like one brand. You can start with a logo and add only the assets that solve a real need.

Questions, answered

Choosing the right identity service.

What is the difference between a logo and a business identity kit?

A logo is the core visual mark for your business. The identity kit is a broader package that includes a logo, business card, letterhead and essential brand assets.

How much does logo design cost?

The listed Swayphics price for logo design is R250. A broader identity kit is custom quoted according to the agreed deliverables.

Do I need a full identity kit?

Not necessarily. You can start with a logo or discuss the materials your business needs now. We can recommend a scope based on your requirements.

Can the identity be used online and in print?

The intended uses should be discussed before the project begins so the agreed final assets can be prepared for the relevant digital or print contexts.

Can I request business cards and letterheads separately?

Yes. Business card design and business letterhead design are also listed as individual Swayphics services.
