Why identity matters
Customers experience your brand across every touchpoint, not just the logo.

Your logo, colours, typography, business cards and documents all contribute to how consistently your business presents itself. When those pieces feel disconnected, customers may have a less coherent impression of the business.

A useful identity gives you a visual foundation you can apply across the materials you actually need. You can begin with a logo and build out the wider set of assets as your business grows.

Questions, answered

Before you get started.

How much does logo design cost?

Standalone logo design is R250. A broader identity kit is custom quoted according to the agreed deliverables and intended uses.

What is the difference between a logo and a business identity kit?

A logo is the core visual mark for your business. An identity kit is a wider package that can combine the logo, business card, letterhead and essential visual assets.

Do I need a full identity kit?

Not necessarily. You can start with a logo or another individual design item. We can recommend a practical scope based on what your business needs now.

Can the identity guarantee more sales or brand recognition?

No design can guarantee sales or a particular level of brand recognition. We create a coherent visual foundation for the agreed uses. Recognition grows through consistent use and the wider customer experience.

What if I do not have colours or fonts yet?

That is fine. We will discuss your business, audience and preferences during the brief, then agree the creative direction that fits the selected scope.

Can I use the final assets online and in print?

We will agree the intended uses before work begins and prepare the final assets for the digital or print contexts included in the project.
