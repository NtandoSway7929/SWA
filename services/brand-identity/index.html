Why identity matters
Your logo, business materials and digital presence should feel like parts of the same business, not separate designs created at different times.

When your logo and business materials look unrelated, customers encounter a different visual impression each time. A considered identity gives your communications a consistent foundation across the places your business appears.

The right starting point may be a single logo or a fuller combination of a logo, business card, letterhead and other agreed brand assets. The aim is to create work that reflects your business and the audience you want to reach.

Questions, answered

Choosing the right identity service.

What is the difference between a logo and a business identity kit?

A logo is the core visual mark for your business. The identity kit is a broader package that includes a logo, business card, letterhead and essential brand assets.

How much does logo design cost?

The listed Swayphics price for logo design is R250. A broader identity kit is custom quoted according to the agreed deliverables.

Do I need a full identity kit?

Not necessarily. You can start with a logo or discuss the materials your business needs now. We can recommend a scope based on your requirements.

Can the identity be used online and in print?

The intended uses should be discussed before the project begins so the agreed final assets can be prepared for the relevant digital or print contexts.

Can I request business cards and letterheads separately?

Yes. Business card design and business letterhead design are also available as individual services.

What do you need from me?

We start with your business name, what you offer, who you want to reach, your design preferences and where the identity will be used. Existing references or examples are useful, but we agree the brief and selected deliverables with you first.

Can I start with just a logo?

Yes. The standalone logo design starts at R250. A broader identity kit is optional and is quoted according to the exact materials you select.
