Why this service matters
Branded apparel should feel like a considered extension of your identity.

Artwork that is too detailed for the chosen print method, poorly positioned or prepared at the wrong dimensions can be difficult to produce. Agreeing the garment, placement and supplier requirements first helps keep the design practical.

We agree the practical requirements and intended use before work begins, so the deliverables match the service you are actually commissioning.

Questions, answered

Before you get started.

What does the R50–R100 price cover?

The design fee depends on the artwork scope agreed for the garment. We confirm the price before starting.

Do you supply the clothing?

No. Garments and sourcing are separate unless a written quote specifically includes them.

Do you print or embroider the designs?

This page covers design artwork. Printing, embroidery and production are handled separately unless agreed in writing.

Can you design for multiple garment types?

Yes. Each garment, placement and any required adaptations should be included in the scope.

What files does my supplier need?

That depends on the production method. Share the supplier's requirements so the artwork can be prepared appropriately.

Can you use my existing logo?

Yes. Supply a usable logo file and any brand guidelines you have.
