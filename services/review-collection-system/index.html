Questions, answered

Before you get started.

Can you guarantee more reviews?

No. Customers decide whether to leave a review. The system makes the invitation and destination easier to access but cannot guarantee participation.

Can the system ask only happy customers for reviews?

We do not set up review gating or suppress negative feedback. Review invitations should be fair and allow customers to share honest experiences.

Which review platforms can be used?

The platform depends on the business and its available review profile. We confirm the destination before setup.

Does it automatically send messages?

Automatic messaging depends on the tools and integrations included in the agreed scope. A manual share workflow may be more appropriate for some businesses.

Are messaging fees included?

Third-party messaging, platform and subscription charges are separate unless expressly included in the quote.

Can I use a QR code?

A QR code can be included if it suits the agreed print or in-person workflow and the destination link is confirmed.
