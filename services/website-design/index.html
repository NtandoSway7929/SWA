Why your website matters
A customer should not have to piece together your offer from scattered posts and messages to understand what you do or how to contact you.

Interested customers look for the basics: what you offer, who you serve, where you operate and how to take the next step. When those details are spread across posts and chats, customers have to do the organising themselves.

The website brings those answers together in one place you control. It gives visitors a clearer route from understanding your offer to contacting your business.

Questions, answered

Before you get started.

How much does a website cost?

Website design and development starts from R1,500. The final quote depends on the number of pages, content, functionality and integrations required for your project.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Responsive design is part of the service. The layout is designed to adapt to common screen sizes, including mobile, tablet and desktop.

Can you add a booking or enquiry feature?

We can discuss the enquiry pathway or booking functionality your business needs. Where a separate booking platform or third-party service is involved, its capabilities and any subscription costs are considered in the project scope.

Do I need to provide the text and images?

We will agree what content is needed during the project discussion. You may provide your business information, copy and images, and the quote can account for any additional content requirements agreed for the project.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short brief with your business details, services, contact information and the action you want customers to take is enough to start the conversation. Existing logos, copy and images are useful where available. We will agree any missing content requirements and confirm what is included before work begins.

Can you guarantee that the website will generate enquiries?

No website can guarantee a particular number of enquiries. The site is designed to explain your offer clearly and make the contact path easier to follow. Results also depend on factors such as demand, your offer and how potential customers reach the website.
