Why your website matters
Your social profiles help people find you. Your website gives them a focused place to understand your business.

When customers are considering a business, they often want to check what it offers, where it operates, how to get in touch and whether it feels right for them. When that information is scattered across different platforms, the next step may not be obvious.

A well-planned website brings the important information together. It gives your business a destination you control and creates a clearer path from first visit to enquiry.

What we build

A digital home with a clear purpose.

The exact scope is agreed before work begins. Depending on your requirements, a Swayphics website project can include:

Responsive page design

A layout designed to adapt across phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Business-focused content layout

Clear sections for your services, products, business information and key messages.

Enquiry pathways

Visible contact options and calls to action that help visitors understand how to take the next step.

Brand-aligned presentation

Visual direction that uses your business identity consistently across the site.

Essential business information

Space for the details customers need, such as location, opening information and service descriptions.

Agreed integrations

Relevant links or tools can be connected where they fit the project scope and platform requirements.

Who it's for

A stronger online presence, at the right stage.

This service is suited to businesses that are starting out and need a professional online home, as well as established businesses whose current website no longer reflects the quality of their work.

It can also suit businesses that rely heavily on social media but need a clearer destination for service information, enquiries or booking links.

Questions, answered

Before you get started.

How much does a website cost?

Website design and development starts from R1,500. The final quote depends on the number of pages, content, functionality and integrations required for your project.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Responsive design is part of the service. The layout is designed to adapt to common screen sizes, including mobile, tablet and desktop.

Can you add a booking or enquiry feature?

We can discuss the enquiry pathway or booking functionality your business needs. Where a separate booking platform or third-party service is involved, its capabilities and any subscription costs are considered in the project scope.

Do I need to provide the text and images?

We will agree what content is needed during the project discussion. You may provide your business information, copy and images, and the quote can account for any additional content requirements agreed for the project.

How do I get started?

Send an enquiry using the button below. Tell us a little about your business and what you need from the website. We will use that information to understand the project and discuss the next step.
