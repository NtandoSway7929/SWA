Why your website matters
A potential customer should not have to piece together your services from posts, captions and message threads.

People coming from a referral, search result or social post need to understand what you offer, whether it is right for them and how to take the next step. When those details are scattered, customers have to do the work of joining the dots.

The Enquiry-Ready Website brings your key information into one focused destination, with a page structure and contact route built around your business. The aim is clarity and a smoother enquiry path, not a promise of a particular number of leads.

Questions, answered

Before you get started.

What website packages do you offer?

Launch Page is R1,500, Essential Business is R4,500, Professional Business is R6,500, Advanced Business is R9,500 and E-commerce starts at R10,000. Each package has defined inclusions and limits. Custom platforms and work outside those limits are quoted separately.

What is included in the website build?

The project can include responsive page design, business-focused content layout, enquiry pathways, brand-aligned presentation, essential business information and agreed integrations. Your quote confirms the exact deliverables.

Do I need to provide the text and images?

We agree content responsibilities during the project discussion. You can provide your business information, copy and images. Copywriting, additional content creation or specialist assets can be scoped separately where required.

Will a website guarantee more enquiries or sales?

No website can guarantee a specific number of enquiries or sales. We build around clear information, mobile usability and a visible next step. Results also depend on your market, offer, traffic and how enquiries are handled.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Responsive design is part of the service. The layout is designed to adapt to common screen sizes, including mobile, tablet and desktop.

Can you add bookings, payments or other advanced features?

Yes, where technically suitable. Appointment booking, advanced forms, customer accounts, memberships, custom integrations and bespoke workflows are assessed separately. A feature is included only when it appears in your written scope.

Are domain and hosting included?

Not by default. Domain registration, hosting, renewals, premium software, paid assets and third-party subscriptions are itemised separately unless your written quote expressly includes them.

How many changes can I request?

Each package includes the revision rounds stated in its description. A revision refines the approved direction; a new concept, new page, new feature or substantial scope change is quoted separately.
