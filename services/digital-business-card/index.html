Questions, answered

Before you get started.

Is this a full website?

No. It is a focused digital profile for contact details and selected links, not a full multi-page website.

Can I include WhatsApp and booking links?

Yes, if you provide the correct destination links and they are compatible with the agreed layout.

Can I use it on Instagram or TikTok?

You can place the profile link in social bios and share it through messages or other channels.

Does the price include a custom domain?

Not by default. Domain, hosting or premium platform costs are separate unless specified in your quote.

Can I update the links later?

The update process depends on the platform and handover arrangement. We will explain what is editable and what would require further assistance.

Will it increase followers or sales?

A digital profile cannot guarantee followers, enquiries or sales. It makes the selected information and next steps easier to access.
