Why your profile matters
When people search locally, they need more than a business name. They need accurate details, useful information and a clear way to reach you.

People comparing nearby options may check your hours, location, services, phone number and website before deciding who to contact. Missing or outdated details can leave them uncertain or force them to search elsewhere for the answer.

A complete profile gives people a more useful picture of your business when they encounter it on Google Search and Maps. It improves the information you present; it does not control Google rankings or guarantee that more people will find you.

Questions, answered

What to know before setup.

Will this guarantee that my business ranks first on Google?

No. Google determines how profiles appear in Search and Maps. Swayphics can help set up accurate, complete business information, but cannot guarantee rankings or placement.

Do I need to own or access the Google account?

The business owner should retain control of the profile. We will discuss the appropriate access or invitation process before any work begins. Do not share your password with us.

Can you help if my business already has a profile?

Yes. The service can cover review and optimisation of an existing profile, depending on its current state and the access available.

What information should I prepare?

Prepare your correct business name, category, contact details, address or service area, opening hours, website and relevant business images where available.

How much does the service cost?

The one-time setup or optimisation price is R500–R900. The final amount depends on the current state of the profile and the work agreed before we begin.

What do you need from me?

Prepare your correct business name, category, contact details, address or service area, opening hours, website and useful business images where available. The business owner should provide the appropriate profile access or invitation. Do not share your password.

Will this guarantee more calls or a higher Google ranking?

No. We improve the accuracy and completeness of the profile information you present. Google decides search placement and customers decide which businesses to contact. We do not promise rankings or a set number of calls.
