Why your profile matters
Local customers need accurate answers before they decide to call, visit or enquire.

A customer ready to visit or enquire should not have to guess whether you are open, what services you offer or which contact details are current. Missing or inconsistent information can create friction before the conversation starts.

We organise the profile information and available fields you control, giving customers a clearer picture of your business on Google Search and Maps. Google retains control of rankings, display and verification decisions.

Who it's for

For local businesses customers need to evaluate before visiting.

This service is for South African local businesses whose customers need accurate information before visiting or making contact.

It is especially useful for salons, beauty and wellness providers, restaurants, professional practices and other service businesses serving a defined area. We can set up a new profile or improve an existing one that is incomplete, outdated or no longer reflects your offer.

Questions, answered

What to know before setup.

Will this guarantee that my business ranks first on Google?

No. Google determines how profiles appear in Search and Maps. Swayphics can help set up accurate, complete business information, but cannot guarantee rankings or placement.

Do I need to own or access the Google account?

The business owner should retain control of the profile. We will discuss the appropriate access or invitation process before any work begins. Do not share your password with us.

Can you help if my business already has a profile?

Yes. The service can cover review and optimisation of an existing profile, depending on its current state and the access available.

What information should I prepare?

Prepare your correct business name, category, contact details, address or service area, opening hours, website and relevant business images where available.

How much does the service cost?

The current price range is R500–R900 for one-time setup and optimisation. The final amount depends on the work required.
