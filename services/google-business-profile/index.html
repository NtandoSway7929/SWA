Why your profile matters
Before a local customer calls, visits or books, they need accurate details and a clear next step.

People searching locally may check your category, services, location, opening hours, phone number and website before deciding whether to contact you. Missing or inconsistent details leave basic questions unanswered.

A well-organised Google Business Profile helps present those details across Google Search and Maps. It can support a clearer first impression, but Google controls search placement, eligibility and verification.

Questions, answered

Before you get started.

Will this guarantee that my business ranks first on Google?

No. Google determines how profiles appear in Search and Maps. We can help make your profile more complete and accurate, but cannot guarantee rankings, placement, views, calls or enquiries.

Can you help if my business already has a profile?

Yes. We can review the current profile and improve the agreed details, depending on its state and the access available.

Do I need to share my Google password?

No. The business owner should retain control of the profile. We will discuss an appropriate access or invitation process. Do not send your password.

What information should I prepare?

Have your correct business name, category, phone number, website, opening hours, address or service area, service details and relevant business photos ready. We will confirm any additional information needed for your setup.

Does the service include ongoing posts and review management?

The listed price covers the agreed setup or optimisation work, not ongoing profile management. Regular posts, review responses or continued updates can be discussed as separate work.

How much does setup cost?

The current one-time setup and optimisation range is R500–R900. The final price depends on the profile's current state and the work required.
