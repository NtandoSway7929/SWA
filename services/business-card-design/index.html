Questions, answered

Before you get started.

Does the price include printing?

No. The listed R100 is for design. Printing, delivery and any printer charges are separate unless expressly included in a written quote.

Can you design both sides?

The layout and number of sides will be confirmed in the scope before work begins.

What information should I provide?

Your business name, contact details, logo and any social, website or location details you want included.

Can you create a card without an existing logo?

Yes. We can discuss a simple text-led layout or quote the logo work separately.

Can you use my printer's specifications?

Yes. Share the printer's dimensions, bleed, colour and file requirements before the artwork is finalised.

Will the card guarantee more customers?

No design can guarantee sales. The card is intended to present your details clearly and make it easier for someone to contact you.
