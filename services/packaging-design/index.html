Why this service matters
Your packaging needs to do more than look attractive. It needs to present the product and its information clearly.

A label or pack design that does not fit the physical format, omits important supplied information or feels disconnected from the brand can create avoidable production and presentation problems. The design brief should establish the packaging format and required content before artwork begins.

We agree the practical requirements and intended use before work begins, so the deliverables match the service you are actually commissioning.

Questions, answered

Before you get started.

Does R150 include printing or packaging?

No. The listed starting price is for design only. Printing, materials, packaging manufacture and delivery are separate.

Do I need a dieline?

If your supplier provides a dieline or template, share it before design starts. If not, we will confirm what dimensions and format information are available.

Can you design multiple product variants?

The number of variants and the changes required must be agreed in the quote because each variant can affect the design scope.

Can you write the ingredients or compliance information?

You must supply and verify the product, ingredient, warning and regulatory information. We can arrange the supplied copy in the design but do not certify its legal compliance.

Can you design a label only?

Yes. The scope can be limited to a label or another agreed packaging component.

Will the design guarantee product sales?

No. Packaging design cannot guarantee sales. It provides a considered visual presentation for your product.
