Questions, answered

Before you get started.

Does the setup include a monthly AI subscription?

No. Third-party platform subscriptions, usage charges and paid integrations are separate unless a quote expressly includes them.

Will AI answer every customer question correctly?

No. AI can misunderstand or produce incorrect responses. The workflow should use approved business information and route uncertain or sensitive enquiries to a person.

Which channels can it work on?

The available channels depend on the selected platform and its integrations. We confirm compatibility before agreeing the scope.

Can it make bookings or take payments?

Only where a compatible integration is included in the agreed scope. Those actions are not assumed as part of the basic reply setup.

Can I control what it says?

We agree the business information, tone and response boundaries with you. You should review the workflow and keep its information current.

Will it replace staff or guarantee more sales?

No. It is an assistance workflow for routine enquiries, not a replacement for human judgement or a guarantee of sales.
