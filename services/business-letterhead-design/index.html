Questions, answered

Before you get started.

What is included in the R150 price?

The agreed letterhead design and digital artwork. Additional templates or document automation are separate unless included in writing.

Can I use it for quotes and proposals?

The design can be prepared for the document uses agreed in the brief.

Do you write the content of my documents?

This service covers the letterhead layout, not drafting legal, financial or business document content.

Can I provide an existing logo?

Yes. Supply your logo and any brand guidelines so the design can follow your existing identity.

Can you make it editable?

Tell us which software you use. We will confirm the suitable file format and editable scope before starting.

Does the price include printing?

No. Printing and delivery are separate unless a quote expressly includes them.
