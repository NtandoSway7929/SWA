01 · Who we are

Swayphics is a creative and design studio providing branding, visual design, digital experiences and related business services.

For purposes of this Privacy Policy, Swayphics is referred to as “Swayphics”, “we”, “us” or “our”.

02 · Information we collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when interacting with our website or communicating with Swayphics.

Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • The service you are interested in.
  • Information contained in your project enquiry.
  • Information voluntarily submitted with a testimonial, including your name, business, role, review, rating and optional image.
  • Information you choose to provide when communicating with us through email, WhatsApp or social media.

We aim to collect information that is relevant and reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is requested.

03 · How we use information

Swayphics may use personal information to:

  • Respond to project enquiries and requests.
  • Understand the services a prospective client requires.
  • Communicate with clients and prospective clients.
  • Provide and manage requested services.
  • Process and manage voluntarily submitted testimonials.
  • Publish an approved testimonial where the person has chosen to provide information for that purpose.
  • Maintain, secure and improve our website and services.
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

Swayphics does not sell personal information to third parties.

04 · Sharing information

We do not sell or rent personal information.

Personal information may be made available to trusted service providers where this is reasonably necessary to operate the website, process enquiries, provide services or maintain technical infrastructure.

Information may also be disclosed where required or permitted by applicable law, or where necessary to protect our rights, users, systems or property.

05 · Service providers

Parts of the Swayphics website infrastructure use third-party technology providers to store and process information submitted through website functionality.

Our website currently uses Supabase infrastructure for certain database and backend functions, including website enquiry and testimonial functionality.

Third-party providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services. Their own terms and privacy practices may also apply.

06 · Retention of information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or comply with legal obligations.

Where information is no longer reasonably required, we may securely delete or otherwise dispose of it, subject to applicable retention requirements.

07 · Security

Swayphics takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

If Swayphics becomes aware of a security compromise affecting personal information, we will take appropriate steps in accordance with applicable law.

08 · Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Object to certain processing of your information.
  • Request deletion of information where applicable.
  • Ask questions about how your information is processed.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator where you believe your privacy rights have been unlawfully interfered with.

To make a privacy-related request, contact Swayphics using the details provided below.

The Information Regulator provides official mechanisms for POPIA complaints and security-compromise reporting.

09 · Third-party services and links

The Swayphics website may contain links to third-party platforms including Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp and other external services.

Once you leave the Swayphics website and interact with a third-party platform, that platform's own privacy policy and terms may apply.

Swayphics is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites and platforms.

10 · Children's information

Our services and website are intended primarily for businesses, organisations and individuals seeking professional creative and digital services.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to the provision of our services.

11 · Changes to this policy

Swayphics may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, technology or legal obligations.

The latest version will be published on this page, together with the date on which it was last updated.
